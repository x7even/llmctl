A downtrain on the slot/riser link is physical: try a full cold power-off (a warm
reboot may not power-cycle the slot), then reseat the card and any riser at both
ends, then swap the card into a known-good slot to tell a bad card from a bad slot.
Check max_link_speed too - if it is also low, something is capping the slot in BIOS.
Treat every tensor-parallel benchmark taken in this state as invalid.
